Privacy Policy

Ayana Studios ("we", "us", or "our") operates the ABC Kids Learning mobile application (the "App").

ABC Kids Learning is an educational application designed primarily for children. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, how it is used, how third-party services may process information, and the choices available to parents and users.

By using ABC Kids Learning, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Summary for Parents & Reviewers
  • No Registration: No account, name, or email required from children.

  • No Microphones: Audio output only; no voice recording or camera access.
  • Parent Zone: Adult-gated access to settings & parent features.

1 Children's Privacy

ABC Kids Learning is designed primarily for young children and is intended to provide educational activities in a child-friendly environment.

We do not require children to create an account, provide their name, email address, phone number, date of birth, photographs, voice recordings, or other directly identifying information to use the App.

The App does not provide child-to-child communication, chat, social networking, public profiles, user-generated content uploads, or sharing features.

Because the App is directed toward children, we take children's privacy seriously and design the App to minimize the collection of personal information.

2 Information We Do Not Directly Collect

ABC Kids Learning does not provide forms or account-registration features that ask users to submit:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Date of birth or age
  • Home or precise location
  • Contacts
  • Photos or videos
  • Camera data
  • Microphone or voice recordings
  • Payment or financial information
  • Passwords or authentication information

The App does not use user accounts, authentication, cloud profiles, or user-uploaded content.

3 Information That May Be Processed Automatically

Although we do not ask children to provide personal information, certain technical and usage information may be processed automatically by third-party services used by the App.

Depending on the service and device, this may include:

  • IP address
  • App installation or app-instance identifiers
  • Device model
  • Operating system and version
  • App version
  • Crash and diagnostic information
  • Information about interactions with the App
  • Advertising and ad-request information

This information is used primarily to operate, maintain, analyze, secure, and improve the App and to provide advertising where applicable.

The App does not intentionally collect a child's name or other directly identifying information for its own user profiles.

4 Firebase Analytics

ABC Kids Learning uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with the App and to help us improve educational content and app functionality.

Analytics information may include events such as:

  • Categories viewed
  • Games started
  • Games completed
  • Games replayed
  • Games exited
  • Hints used
  • Correct and incorrect answers
  • Game scores
  • Game duration

The App does not intentionally attach a child's name, email address, or other directly identifying information to these analytics events.

Firebase may automatically process technical information such as app-instance identifiers and device-related information as part of providing its analytics service.

5 Firebase Crashlytics

We use Firebase Crashlytics to identify and diagnose technical problems and crashes.

Crashlytics may process information such as:

  • Crash stack traces
  • Exception information
  • Device model
  • Operating system version
  • App version
  • Technical diagnostic information

We do not intentionally use Crashlytics to collect a child's name, email address, phone number, photographs, voice recordings, or precise location.

Crashlytics helps us identify technical problems and improve the stability and reliability of ABC Kids Learning.

6 Advertising and Google AdMob

ABC Kids Learning uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

The App may display:

  • Banner advertisements
  • Rewarded advertisements

A rewarded advertisement may be presented when a user chooses to watch an advertisement to unlock certain optional content, such as a coloring activity.

The App does not require a user to provide personal information in order to view or use the educational content.

Advertising services may automatically process technical information, including IP address and information associated with an advertising or ad request, in accordance with the advertising provider's policies.

Because ABC Kids Learning is designed for children, advertising is configured and used in accordance with applicable Google Play Families requirements and applicable advertising policies.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, users may review Google's applicable privacy documentation.

7 Child-Directed Advertising

ABC Kids Learning is designed for children. We take steps to configure advertising in accordance with applicable requirements for child-directed applications.

We do not intentionally use the App to create personalized advertising profiles based on a child's name, email address, precise location, or similar directly identifying information.

Advertising functionality may still involve technical information necessary for an advertisement to be requested and delivered.

Parents should also review the applicable policies and privacy information provided by Google and its advertising services.

8 Local Data Stored on the Device

Some information is stored locally on the user's device so that the App can remember settings and learning progress.

Examples include:

  • Sound settings
  • Game high scores
  • Learning progress
  • Current levels
  • Selected items
  • Smart Coloring progress
  • Smart Coloring unlock status

This information is stored locally and is not used to create an online child profile.

The App does not use a cloud database to synchronize a child's learning profile between devices.

9 Internet and Network Connections

ABC Kids Learning is designed to work primarily as an offline educational application.

An internet connection may be used for services such as:

  • Delivering advertisements through Google AdMob
  • Sending analytics information through Firebase Analytics
  • Sending crash and diagnostic information through Firebase Crashlytics

The App does not operate its own user account server or custom backend for storing children's personal information.

10 Permissions

The current version of ABC Kids Learning requires network-related access for services such as advertising and analytics.

The App does not request access to:

  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • Contacts
  • Calendar
  • Precise location
  • User photographs or gallery
  • Phone number
  • SMS or call logs

The Android Advertising ID permission is also not included in the current application configuration.

11 Audio

ABC Kids Learning contains educational sounds, phonics audio, music, and other audio playback.

The App uses audio playback only.

The App does not record or transmit children's voices and does not require microphone access.

12 Camera, Photos, and User Uploads

ABC Kids Learning does not use the camera, photo gallery, or user-upload functionality.

Images and educational artwork used by the App are bundled with the application or otherwise provided as part of the App's content.

The App does not upload photographs, drawings, videos, or other user-generated media.

13 Location Information

ABC Kids Learning does not request or collect precise location information.

The App does not use GPS or location services to provide its educational features.

14 Social Features and Communication

ABC Kids Learning does not provide:

  • Chat
  • Messaging
  • Child-to-child communication
  • Public profiles
  • Social feeds
  • User-generated public posts
  • Content sharing between users
  • Community features

The App also does not allow children to communicate directly with strangers through the App.

15 Parent Zone

ABC Kids Learning includes a Parent Zone designed to help restrict access to certain parent-oriented functionality.

Access to the Parent Zone uses an adult-action mechanism, including a mathematical question and/or long-press interaction.

The Parent Zone may contain parent-oriented functionality such as subscription or purchase-related screens.

The current App does not require children to create accounts or provide personal information to access its core educational activities.

16 Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell children's personal information.

The App may transmit information to third-party service providers when necessary for the services described in this Privacy Policy, including:

  • Google AdMob — advertising services
  • Google Firebase Analytics — app usage analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash and diagnostic reporting

These services may process technical or usage information in accordance with their respective terms and privacy policies.

We do not operate a separate database containing children's names, email addresses, phone numbers, photographs, or profiles.

17 Data Retention

Information stored locally by the App, such as learning progress and settings, generally remains on the device until:

  • The user deletes the information through available App functionality, if provided;
  • The App's stored data is cleared through the device settings;
  • The App is uninstalled; or
  • The relevant information is overwritten or reset by the App.

Information processed by third-party services such as Google Firebase and Google AdMob is subject to the respective provider's retention and deletion practices.

Because ABC Kids Learning does not create user accounts, we do not maintain a cloud-based child account that can be accessed across devices.

18 Data Deletion and Parental Requests

Since ABC Kids Learning does not require user accounts and does not maintain a cloud-based personal profile for children, there is generally no child account for us to delete.

Parents may delete locally stored App data by clearing the App's data through the device's operating-system settings or by uninstalling the App.

If you have a privacy-related question or believe that information associated with your use of the App requires review or deletion, you may contact us at:

Email Privacy Contact: ayanastudios22@gmail.com

We will review requests and respond as appropriate.

19 Data Security

We take reasonable measures to limit the collection and use of information and to protect information processed through the App.

Because the App relies on third-party services such as Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics, and Firebase Crashlytics, information transmitted to those services is also subject to their security practices and policies.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

20 Third-Party Privacy Policies

ABC Kids Learning uses third-party services whose privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Users and parents may review the privacy information provided by:

  • Google
  • Google AdMob
  • Google Firebase
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics

We recommend that parents review the applicable third-party privacy policies to understand how those services process technical, advertising, analytics, and diagnostic information.

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's functionality, data practices, third-party services, or applicable requirements change.

When we make changes, we will update the Effective Date at the beginning of this Privacy Policy.

We encourage parents and users to periodically review this page for the latest version.
